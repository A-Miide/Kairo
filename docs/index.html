Installing, step by step

macOS

  1. Open the DMG and drag KAIRO into Applications.
  2. Double-click it. KAIRO is signed and checked by Apple, so it opens like any other app.

Windows

  1. Run the .msi. Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”, as it does for apps it hasn’t seen often.
  2. Click More info → Run anyway. You only do this once.

Linux

  1. Debian/Ubuntu: sudo dpkg -i KAIRO_*.deb, then open it from your apps menu.
  2. Fedora/RHEL: sudo rpm -i KAIRO-*.rpm

Outputs

Every screen gets its own look

The projector, the livestream, the lobby TV and the stage monitor each get their own theme, from one verse going up once.

  • Screens, NDI, Syphon or Spout, and Blackmagic DeckLink
  • A monitor of every output: what’s live, what’s next
  • A screen you saved comes back when it’s plugged in again
Director mode

Cameras and scripture, one switcher

Turn on Director mode and KAIRO becomes a switcher for your cameras, with the verse placed beside the preacher.

  • Preview and Program, with Space to send
  • Layouts that frame the verse beside the camera, one key each
  • Stream and record what the output shows, straight from KAIRO
Requirements

What you need

A computer

A Mac with macOS 12 or later (offline listening on Apple Silicon needs macOS 14), Windows 10 or 11, or Ubuntu, Debian or Fedora. An ordinary office PC with 8 GB of memory is plenty.

The preacher’s sound

A feed from your sound desk or a USB audio interface. The computer’s own microphone is fine for trying it.

Internet

Only to download KAIRO and its offline speech model. After that, services run without it.

Optional

A screen or projector per output, a Deepgram key for cloud listening, NDI Tools for ProPresenter or OBS, a DeckLink card, cameras for Director mode.
